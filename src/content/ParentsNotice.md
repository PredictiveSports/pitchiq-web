# PitchIQ — Information for Parents and Adult Sponsors

**Last Updated: July 25, 2026**

---

This notice summarizes how PitchIQ handles information for **children under 13** and the rights available to their parents, legal guardians, and authorized coaches ("Adult Sponsors"). It supplements our full [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) and [Terms of Service](/terms/), which govern your use of the Services.

PitchIQ is a **mixed-audience** service used by baseball and softball players of varying ages — including children under 13 — along with their families and coaches. We built the Services around data minimization for young users and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA") with respect to users under 13.

---

## How a Child account works

A child does not create or hold their own account. An Adult Sponsor (18 or older) creates the account using the Adult Sponsor's own email and password, agrees to our Terms of Service, and supervises the child's use of the Services. A neutral age screen determines, from the date of birth entered, that a child-appropriate, parent-controlled account applies.

## What we collect about a child

**We do not collect personal information from a child.** We do not ask a child for their name, email address, phone number, photograph, precise location, or contact information. Within the Services a child is identified only by a **non-identifying display name**.

We do collect the gameplay and progress information a child generates while using the Services — for example, scenarios played, pitch calls, outcomes, and scores — and limited technical data (such as app version and device type) needed to operate and secure the Services. We use this information only for the internal operation, support, security, and improvement of the Services.

## How we use and share it

- We do **not** use a child's information for targeted advertising.
- We do **not** sell a child's information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- We disclose it only to the service providers that help us operate the Services (such as authentication and hosting), or as required by law or to protect safety, as described in our Privacy Policy.

## Your rights as a parent or Adult Sponsor

At any time, you may:

- **review** the personal information associated with the child's account;
- **request that we correct or delete** that information; and
- **refuse to permit further collection or use** of the child's information, by deleting the account.

You can delete the account and its associated data from within the app (Profile → delete account) or by contacting us. Because we do not collect a child's own contact information, we work with you through the Adult Sponsor's account and contact details to verify and honor these requests.
